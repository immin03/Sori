Open Korea 101 in Sori

A phone on the table. Where did its owner go?

A small cafe moment opens a bigger conversation about trust.

In some Seoul cafes you may notice a laptop, a bag or a phone on a table while its owner is briefly away. For visitors used to carrying everything even to the bathroom, it can be an unexpectedly memorable detail.

Sometimes the person is ordering. Sometimes they have stepped away for a moment. An object can also signal that a seat is taken. It is an informal habit, not a written rule shared by every cafe.

The interesting question is not whether every belonging is safe. No place can promise that. It is how people interpret the same small scene: one visitor sees a risk, another sees a seat being held, and someone else barely notices.

If you are visiting, choose the level of care you are comfortable with and keep passports, valuables and essential belongings with you. You can enjoy noticing a custom without having to copy it.

And if a busy cafe’s seating system is unclear, ask the staff before settling in. The best cultural discoveries often start with a simple question.
