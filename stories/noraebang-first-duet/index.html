Open Korea 101 in Sori

The easiest line to sing at a Korean karaoke night

You know the chorus. Your friend knows the verses. Here are the words that get you both to the microphone.

Imagine that a friend has put your favorite Korean song in the queue. You take the microphone with complete confidence. Then the introduction ends and it dawns on you that every clip you've heard online starts forty seconds further into the song. Luckily, there's a second microphone.

같이 부를까요? (gachi bureulkkayo) means “Shall we sing together?” It's a useful first line because it gives your friend a part in the evening. A 노래방 (noraebang) is literally a singing room. In a private room, you and your friends choose your own music. Someone can sing a verse, someone else can wait for the chorus, and the person who picked the song can handle that impossible high note.

If you're picturing nothing but dance hits, the old song charts have a surprise. Circle Chart researcher Kim Jin-woo looked at Korean karaoke rankings from 2014 through 2023. Seventy of the hundred highest-ranked songs were ballads. Yoon Jong-shin's 2017 breakup ballad “Like It” came first. So the person reaching for the remote may have a whole story to sing before anyone gets to your favorite choreography.

When your song comes around again, try 후렴은 알아요 (huryeomeun arayo), “I know the chorus.” Hand the first verse to your friend. Listen for how the singer stretches the vowels, then come in on the bit you both remember. The same exchange works at home with a friend, a playlist and two spoons standing in for microphones. Which song would you choose if a friend took the second microphone?
