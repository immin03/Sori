Open Korea 101 in Sori

Your first Korean journey starts before the city.

A hotel pin can make an airport arrival feel a little less complicated.

There is a particular feeling to arriving in a new country: excitement, tiredness and a phone full of information you suddenly need at the same time. Before choosing what to visit, save where you are sleeping.

If you arrive at Incheon, choose the correct terminal. AREX connects the airport with Seoul, and the all-stop service connects at places including Hongik University and Gimpo. An express train to Seoul Station is not automatically the most convenient option for every hotel.

Gimpo has airport rail and Metro 5 and 9 connections. For Busan’s Gimhae Airport, light rail connects with Metro 2 at Sasang and Metro 3 at Daejeo. The last connection to your accommodation still matters.

Schedules, luggage, accessibility and how you feel can all change the best choice. Open current directions and compare a train connection with other options. A static travel tip cannot promise a live departure.

Once the first journey is settled, the city feels a little more manageable. The cafes and beautiful streets can wait until you have put down the bag.
