The streets of Hongdae are a stage

Follow the music through one of Seoul's most expressive neighbourhoods.

On an evening in Hongdae, you might hear music before you can see the performance. A crowd gathers around a singer or a dance group, then disperses to another street. It is a lively way to encounter Seoul's creative culture.

The neighbourhood grew around Hongik University and has become associated with independent music, art, fashion and nightlife. Visit Seoul describes the R2 pedestrian area as a place for busking, where performers and passersby share the street.

You can enjoy Hongdae without buying a ticket or following a fixed itinerary. Browse a small shop, stop for a snack and spend a little time listening. Keep pathways clear and be considerate of other people enjoying the performance.

If you are exploring with a new friend, 사진 좀 찍어 주실래요? is a polite way to ask someone to take your photo. You can practise it before your next trip.

Hongdae changes with the time of day. A quiet afternoon can turn into an evening full of lights and music. Visit the neighbourhood at your own pace and see what catches your attention.
