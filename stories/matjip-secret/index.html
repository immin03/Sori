What makes a Korean restaurant a 맛집?

A word that tells you where to eat, and why Koreans love sharing good places.

If a Korean friend suggests visiting a 맛집 (matjip), you are probably about to eat something good. 맛 means flavour, and 집 literally means house. Together, the word describes a place known for serving good food.

A 맛집 can be a tiny family-run restaurant or a celebrated modern dining room. Recommendations often travel through friends, colleagues and online communities. A queue might catch your attention, but the real test is whether the food suits your own taste.

Gwangjang Market in Jongno is a fun place to try the idea for yourself. It is known for dishes such as 빈대떡 (mung bean pancakes), 꼬마김밥 (small seaweed rice rolls) and 떡볶이 (spicy rice cakes). Choose something you can share, then wander to another stall if you are still hungry.

When asking for a neighbourhood full of good food, try 맛집 골목이 어디예요? It means “Where is the alley with good restaurants?” If you have already found a restaurant, 추천 메뉴가 있나요? asks whether there is a recommended dish.

Next time you discover a place you love, tell a friend about it. That is part of the pleasure of finding a 맛집. The best recommendation is often a small story about what you ate and why you want to go back.
