Open Korea 101 in Sori

One coffee. Your first little win in Korean.

You do not need a perfect sentence to order your usual.

You reach the counter. You know what you want. Then the menu, the queue and the thought of getting the words wrong arrive all at once. A small sentence helps: 아메리카노 한 잔, 주세요. One americano, please.

The useful part is 주세요, juseyo. It makes a request polite and gives you a starting point you can reuse. You are asking for something, not delivering a speech. A pause or a second try is completely fine.

Listen once at a comfortable speed. Say the sentence without the audio. Then listen again and notice where the words run together. That small comparison is more useful than trying to memorise every sound perfectly at once.

At the cafe, the next question might be about the size, temperature or whether you are staying. It is all right to point at the menu or ask for a moment. This phrase is the beginning of a conversation, not a promise that you will understand every reply.

Try it today, even if today’s cafe is your kitchen. Tomorrow, come back and see whether the sentence arrives a little more easily.
