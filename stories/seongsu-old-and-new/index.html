Why Seoul turns old factories into cafés

A slow afternoon in Seongsu reveals a different side of the city.

Walk east from Seoul Forest and the scenery begins to change. Trees give way to brick buildings, workshops and cafés with surprisingly high ceilings. Welcome to Seongsu-dong, a Seoul neighbourhood where industrial history is part of its charm.

Seongsu has long been associated with manufacturing and handmade shoes. As designers, restaurants and cafés moved into the area, many old spaces found new uses. You can still pass a working repair shop on your way to a carefully designed coffee bar.

There is no need to make a long checklist. Start at Seoul Forest, walk toward the café streets, and choose one place that makes you curious. A good afternoon can be as simple as a park, a cup of coffee and a few unexpected shops.

If you would rather keep walking with your drink, say 포장할게요. It means “I'll take it to go.” If you want to stay, you can say 먹고 갈게요, a common way to say you will eat or drink in.

One of Seoul's pleasures is discovering how much a neighbourhood can change within a few streets. Take your time and look up at the buildings as you walk.
