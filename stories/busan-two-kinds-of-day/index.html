Open Korea 101 in Sori

In Busan, choose the kind of day before the places.

A beach morning and a hillside afternoon need room between them.

A list can make every place in a city look equally close. A map quickly tells a different story. In Busan, the beach districts around Haeundae and Gwangalli and the older downtown around Nampo belong to different parts of the day.

Try choosing a mood first. A coastal day might leave time for the beach and a cafe. A downtown day might bring together markets and a neighbourhood walk. You do not have to collect every landmark to feel that you have been somewhere.

Gamcheon’s hillside lanes ask for a different pace from a flat promenade. It is also a residential neighbourhood. Follow visitor routes, keep your voice down and allow time for slopes, stairs and transport.

Put your hotel on the map before adding the third or fourth stop. The journey back is part of the plan, too. Compare actual transport options in your map app rather than judging a trip only by a straight line.

Then invite a friend. Sometimes the most useful part of a shared itinerary is discovering that both of you would rather sit with a view for another half hour.
