# Korea 101 editorial policy

Korea 101 by Sori is a cultural newsletter written for curious readers abroad, not a generic travel blog. Each issue should explain a surprising or timely Korean habit from the perspective of Korean everyday life, without inventing first-person memories or presenting subjective customs as universal.

## Issue checklist

- An intriguing title and subtitle, in natural, accessible English. No em dashes.
- Aim for 800–1,200 words including language learning, approximately five to eight minutes. Depth matters more than length alone.
- Begin with a specific, observable moment; explain why it happens. Include verified historical periods, named examples, and useful distinctions or exceptions where relevant.
- Organize the story with four or five readable subheadings. Include three or more relevant editorial images with accurate captions and permission or credits. Context images and AI illustrations must be labeled; never imply they depict a specific place/event if they do not.
- Include two to four natural expressions with Hangul, pronunciation, English meaning, example sentences, audio, and a related practice link. End with a thoughtful reader question.
- Use primary sources where possible and link the original Sori newsletter if developing a previously covered subject. Keep original dates and URLs.
- Check all event dates, local examples, prices and closures near publication; distinguish planned dates, estimates, and confirmed information. Do not assume last year's exhibition will happen again.

## Timely editorial calendar

| Period | Ideas |
| --- | --- |
| November | First snow, autumn-to-winter changes, kimjang |
| December | Christmas dates and cakes, winter snacks, year-end gatherings |
| January | New Year sunrise, winter markets, office greetings |
| Lunar New Year | Seollal, tteokguk, sebae, changing age conventions (verify lunar date) |
| Spring | Cherry blossoms, university festivals, Children's Day |
| Summer | Monsoon, bingsu, boknal, summer holidays |
| Autumn | Chuseok, fall foliage, university festivals, seasonal markets |

Before each issue, check the actual date and current cultural or seasonal developments. Scheduled editorial suggestions are not an automatic publication commitment. Maintain the issue source in content/korea101.cjs and run node scripts/build-stories.cjs.

Publication policy (21 Nov 2026): keep the six founder-published originals and the newly approved first-snow and Christmas issues visible. The nine earlier draft rewrites were retired from public listings and share previews; their earlier versions are retained in repository history. Publish future issues only when they meet the editorial checklist and the founder's content direction. Keep a draft outside the public site's sources until ready to publish.
